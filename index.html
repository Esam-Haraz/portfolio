IBM HR Analytics — Employee Attrition & Performance

End-to-end HR analytics solution evaluating 1,470 employee records across SQL, Excel, and Power BI to pinpoint turnover drivers across overtime, income levels, tenure, and job satisfaction.

1,470 Total Employees
16.12% Attrition Rate (237)
39.76% Sales Rep Attrition
  • Overtime employees leave at a 31% rate compared to 10% for non-overtime staff (3.1x turnover rate).
  • Sales Representatives record the highest attrition rate across all roles at 39.76%, nearly double the next role.
  • Employees who left earned a lower average monthly income ($4,787) compared to retained employees ($6,832).
Recommendation: Cap monthly overtime hours and audit compensation/quota structures for Sales Representatives to reduce early-tenure turnover.

Pizza Sales Performance & Operations Analytics

Retail sales & operations pipeline processing 21,350 orders ($817.86K revenue) using Power Query, SQL date conversion, and a 60-30-10 Power BI dashboard to guide staffing and menu strategy.

$817.86K Total Revenue
21,350 Total Orders
$38.31 Avg Order Value
  • Fridays and Thursdays record the highest weekly order volumes, defining peak kitchen operational windows.
  • The Classic category leads total demand and revenue, while Large (L) size dominates customer preferences.
  • The Thai Chicken Pizza is the top revenue generator, whereas The Brie Carre Pizza records the lowest revenue.
Recommendation: Concentrate kitchen staffing and prep inventory on Thursday/Friday rush hours, and bundle or replace low-performing items like The Brie Carre Pizza.

Supply Chain & Shipment Deliveries Analysis

Analyzed 25,000 shipment records using PostgreSQL and Excel to uncover delay drivers, cost scaling, and partner performance—resolving duplicate/decimal primary key issues via SERIAL keys.

25,000 Total Shipments
26.68% Overall Delay Rate
41.45% Stormy Delay Rate
  • Stormy weather produces a 41.45% delay rate compared to 16.02% in cold weather — nearly a 2.5x difference.
  • Express delivery recorded the lowest average customer rating (2.72 / 5) despite being the fastest shipping mode.
  • Delivery cost scales linearly with distance from 239.64 (0–50 km) up to 1,365.76 (200+ km).
Recommendation: Build weather buffer times into SLA estimates during stormy seasons, and investigate whether unmet speed expectations explain the low 2.72 Express rating.
